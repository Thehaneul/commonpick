Project name: CommonPick

Project description:
CommonPick helps two participants choose a meeting format that fits both of
their stated needs. GenLayer validators assess three fixed options from their
plain-language requirements. The contract finds the shared suitable options
and allows either participant to select only one of those options, preserving
the original assessments and record hashes.

Why GenLayer:
Interpreting everyday requirements needs judgment. Validators independently
compare the needs with the option descriptions and check the explanation. Their
agreed verdicts determine which choices the contract permits, without relying
on one organiser's AI response.

What makes it different:
It combines two members' compatible-option sets into a shared choice. Our earlier
projects covered source origins, experimental separation, client scope changes,
translated notices, learning progression, stock holds and task routing.
